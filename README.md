A live 3D map of Singapore buses, with journey planning by bus, MRT and LRT.

**Open the app:** https://zaniestp.github.io/sg-bus-map/

It works in any modern phone or computer browser. There's nothing to install. On a phone you can add it to your home screen from the browser's Share or ⋮ menu.

## 1. Quick start

1. Open the link above.
2. When the browser asks, **allow location**. The app uses it to find stops and stations near you and to follow your bus. It stays on your phone.
3. Type a bus number (e.g. `168`), a stop, an MRT station or a postal code in the search box. You can also tap **🧭** to plan a journey.
4. **Live bus times need an access code.** Request one from **zaniestp** on GitHub: https://github.com/zaniestp. Then tap ⚙ → **Access code**, type it in and tap **Save**. Without a code, the map, directions and stations still work; only the live arrival times show "Live times not available".

## 3. Searching

Type in the search box at the top. Results appear as you type:

| You type | You get |
|---|---|
| `168`, `903M` | The bus route on the map with live buses |
| `46009`, `Woodlands Int` | A bus stop with its next arrivals |
| `NS9`, `te2`, `Dhoby Ghaut`, `woodlands mrt` | 🚇 MRT / LRT stations – by name or station code |
| `Tampines St 82` | A road, with all its bus stops |
| `520844`, `844 Tampines St 82`, `Albert Complex` | Addresses and buildings |

Your ⭐ favourites always show first.

**Tip:** long-press any building on the map to see its name, block number, street and postal code.

## 4. Bus stops and stations

**Bus stop:** tap a stop on the map or in the search results. You'll see the next buses with their live times. From there you can:
- tap a bus to see its route,
- tap **🧭 Directions here** to plan a trip to the stop,
- tap **☆ Save stop** to save it as a favourite.

**MRT / LRT station:** the station card shows:
- its lines and codes, e.g. `NS9 · North South Line`, `TE2 · Thomson-East Coast Line`,
- every **exit**, with the bus stops next to it and their buses. Tap an exit to zoom to it,
- **🧭 Directions here**, **🧭 From here** and **☆ Save**.

## 5. 🧭 Directions

Tap **🧭** at the top. The planner has three tabs:

**① Start** – where you're starting from:
- 📍 My location, a favourite, a bus stop, an MRT station, or any address or building.
- For an address, the app shows the map with nearby stops highlighted. **Tap the stop you want**, or pick **☰ List**, or let the app choose the best stop (**✨**).
- You can change your mind at any time. Tap the tab again and choose something else.

**② End** – where you're going. You choose it the same way.

**③ Routes** – up to 5 suggestions, best first.

### Reading a route

Each route shows its parts in order:

| Sign | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🚶 4 | Walk, about 4 minutes |
| 🚌 903 / 903M | Bus – any of these buses will do |
| 🚌 129 \| 298 | Last bus – either one, but they stop at different stops |
| 🚇 NSL | MRT, in its line colour |
| 🚈 BPLRT | LRT |

MRT line colours: 🔴 NSL North South · 🟢 EWL East West · 🟣 NEL North East · 🟠 CCL Circle · 🔵 DTL Downtown · 🟤 TEL Thomson-East Coast · ⚪ LRT lines are grey.

The card also shows the time, roughly when you'll arrive, the walking time and when the next bus you can still catch arrives.

### Route options

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| ⚡ Fastest | Quickest overall |
| 🔁 Fewest | Fewest changes |
| 🚶 Least walk | Least walking |
| ⏰ Peak buses | Include peak-hour-only buses (City Direct 6xx and express "e" buses). Routes using a peak bus that isn't running now are greyed out under **⏸ Not running now** |
| 🚇 MRT | Include MRT and LRT. **Turn it off for buses only.** The app remembers your choice |

### Route details

Tap a route to see every step:
- where to walk, and which **station exit** to use,
- where to board, how many stops or stations, which direction the train is going ("towards Marina South Pier"), and where to get off,
- live times for the buses,
- your own walking notes for the destination, if you've saved any (see [Favourites](#7-favourites-and-saved-routes)).

**Change a suggested route.** Tap a bus or train step to edit it:
- **☑ / ☐** – tick or untick a bus to keep only the ones you want,
- **🔄 Swap bus / Other line** – take a different bus or line,
- **🏁 Get off elsewhere** – choose a different stop or station. The rest is planned again from there,
- **🚏 Board elsewhere** – choose a different stop.

Below the steps:
- **➕ Go via a stop** – make the route pass through a stop or station you choose,
- **🚫 Avoid a bus / line** – plan again without it, for this trip or always,
- **⭐ Save route** – keep this exact route,
- **▶ Start journey** – step-by-step guidance (see below).

### Train disruptions

If there's an MRT or LRT disruption, a **⚠ red banner** shows what's affected. Routes through the affected stations are left out. Tap **Use the line anyway** if you still want them.

### Night time

Trains run from about 5:30 am to 11:45 pm. Outside these hours the app shows buses only and tells you why.

> Times are estimates. Bus times are live when available. Train times are worked out from the distance between stations.

## 6. I'm on board – bus or train

Tap **🚌** at the top. Choose the tab: **🚌 Bus** or **🚇 MRT / LRT**. The app remembers which one you used last.

### On a bus

1. Enter the bus number you're on.
2. Pick the direction, or let GPS work it out once the bus moves.
3. Choose the stop you're getting off at.
4. The map follows your bus. Your phone **beeps and vibrates** before your stop.

### On a train

1. **Boarded at** – the app suggests the nearest station. Tap ✎ Change to pick another (by name or code, e.g. `NS8`).
2. **Getting off at** – type the station.
3. Tap **Start ▶**.

The train view shows:
- the next station and how many minutes away it is,
- every station on the way with an estimated time,
- **🔔 "Get off at the next station"** – an alert with beep and vibration, and which exit to use.

Underground there's usually no GPS, so the position is estimated from the time. If it's out, **tap the station you're at**, or use **◀ ▶**. When GPS works near a station, the app moves on by itself.

When you arrive, tap **🏁 I'm at …** to see the station's exits and the buses next to them.

If your trip needs a change of line, the app guides you through each line in turn.

### Following a planned journey

After **▶ Start journey** in Directions, the app takes you one step at a time:
- **Bus step:** go to the stop, tap the bus you're waiting for, and it follows that bus and tells you when to get off.
- **Train step:** tap **▶ I'm on the train** once you're on board.
- It moves on to the next bus or train by itself.
- At the end, it shows the walk to your destination, with your walking note if you saved one.

Lost or changed plans? Tap **🔄 Re-plan from here**.

## 7. Favourites and saved routes

**Save a place:** tap **☆ Save** on a stop, station, address or a place in Directions. Choose an icon (🏠 🏫 🏢 …) and a name. Add **details** like a unit number, e.g. `#06-07 to 12`.

**Walking notes:** for a favourite, add a note for each stop you might get off at, e.g. *"Cross the road to Gate 4"*. The note appears at the end of your journey.

**Save a route:** open a route in Directions and tap **⭐ Save route**. Saved routes show at the top of 🧭 Directions.

**Manage them:** ⚙ → **⭐ Favourites & saved routes**. You can reorder (↑), edit (✎), rename or delete them.

**Move to another phone:** favourites are kept on this phone only.
1. Tap **📤 Export (copy)** and send the copied text to your other phone, e.g. by message to yourself.
2. On the other phone, tap **📥 Import** and paste it.

## 8. Map extras – buildings, weather, birds and sounds

All of these are in ⚙ Settings.

- **Theme:** Auto (yellow in the morning, white in the day, dark at night), or choose one.
- **Colour buildings by use:** zoom in close to see homes, shops, schools and offices in different colours, with block numbers and names. Hide the legend with ✕ and bring it back with 🎨.
- **Weather animation:** live rain and clouds from the NEA 2-hour forecast. ⚡ Lightning flashes when lightning is detected nearby. **Preview** cycles through all the effects. You can also dim the weather or turn it off during a trip.
- **Day and night effects:** small orioles and crows on rooftops in the daytime that fly off when startled, and nightjars at night. They don't come out in the rain, but nightjars still call. Tap a bird to hear it. Use the **Bird frequency** slider to see them more or less often.
- **Sounds:** gentle bells, rain and thunder during a trip, plus bird calls (oriole, crow, nightjar). Each one can be switched on or off. There are two volume sliders and **▶ Preview sounds**. During a trip, tap the 🔊 button to mute everything (🔇), and tap again to unmute.

## 9. Tips and troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| A new feature isn't there / app looks old | Check your version: tap ⚙, then tap the **Settings title 5 times**. Close the tab and open the link again |
| "Live times not available" | You need an access code. Request one from zaniestp on GitHub (https://github.com/zaniestp), then enter it in ⚙ → Access code. If you already have one, check it's typed exactly right |
| No sound | Turn the media volume up. On iPhone, also switch off silent mode. During a trip, check the sound button shows 🔊, not 🔇 |
| Location not found | Allow location for your browser in the phone settings, then tap ◎ |
| Train position wrong | Tap the station you're at in the train view |
| A bus in a saved route has changed | Open the route and tap the leg marked ⚠ → Swap |
| Route seems odd | Times are estimates. Try ⚡ / 🔁 / 🚶 or edit the route |

Keep the screen on during a trip. The app tries to stop the phone from sleeping while you're on board.
